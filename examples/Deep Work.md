# Deep Work

Deep Work is a concept coined by Cal Newport, defined as professional activities performed in a state of distraction-free concentration that push cognitive capabilities to their limit.

## Definition

> "Deep work is the ability to focus without distraction on a cognitively demanding task."
> — Cal Newport

## Characteristics

- **Distraction-free** - No interruptions from email, social media, or notifications
- **Cognitively demanding** - Requires full mental engagement
- **Creates value** - Produces meaningful output that is hard to replicate
- **Improves skills** - Pushes abilities to new levels

## Deep Work vs Shallow Work

| Deep Work | Shallow Work |
|-----------|--------------|
| Requires focus | Can be done while distracted |
| Creates new value | Logistical in nature |
| Hard to replicate | Easy to replicate |
| Improves skills | Doesn't improve skills |

## Application to Learning

Deep work is essential for:
- Learning [[Programming Fundamentals]]
- Building a [[Zettelkasten Method]] practice
- Understanding complex topics in [[Computer Science Basics]]

## Strategies

1. **Time blocking** - Schedule specific hours for deep work
2. **Ritualize** - Create consistent starting routines
3. **Embrace boredom** - Train your mind to resist distraction
4. **Quit social media** - Or at least limit it severely
